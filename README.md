## 🧭 About Me

Electronics Engineering student from **Pakistan** 🇵🇰, building expertise across the **full hardware stack** — from circuit design, schematics & PCB layout to firmware, prototyping, testing and debugging, with a strong growing focus on **robotics & automation**.

Alongside engineering, I'm developing **leadership, team coordination & project management** skills — with the long-term goal of becoming an engineer who can both **build technology and lead the teams behind it**.

- 🔭 Currently working on: **ESP32-based IoT systems, UWB tags & 4-layer PCB design**
- 🤖 Moving towards: **Robotics, Automation & Control Systems** *(Open to working on robotics & automation projects and collaborations!)*
- 🌱 Currently learning: **Signal Integrity, RF PCB Design & RTOS**
- 🎯 Goal: **International internships, research & fully funded graduate scholarships**
- ⚡ Philosophy: *Learn the fundamentals. Build real things. Break them. Debug them. Build them better.*
